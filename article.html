Why a single AI readiness score ranks fast garbage above slow craft

Most AI readiness scores would rank a team shipping fast garbage above a team shipping slowly and well.

That is not a flaw in one vendor's assessment. It is what happens any time capability gets collapsed into a single number. Add up thirteen capabilities on a flat scale and a team that piloted all thirteen once and shipped none scores sixty-seven and reads as mature.

A team running four of them in production, with someone reviewing every output before it goes anywhere, scores lower and looks behind.

It flatters the wrong team.

I built the instrument on two axes instead. Leverage asks whether the machine is actually running. Judgment asks whether anything in the process decides what is good enough.

The test is cheap. Ask an assistant the question your best customer asked you last month, and see whether you are in the answer.
